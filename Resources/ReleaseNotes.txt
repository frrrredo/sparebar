Keep a few spare batteries ready for your Codex allowance.

Available Codex usage resets now appear as a horizontal reserve beneath your allowance. Filled batteries stay on the left; confirmed resets used in Sparebar on this Mac stay on the right. Empty reserves say No spares available.

At 10% remaining or less, Use a spare offers an account and reset confirmation. Healthy allowance stays quiet. Sparebar never redeems automatically and always rechecks the provider result.

Interrupted requests keep the same request ID across retries and restarts, helping prevent accidental duplicate use. Reset support depends on your Codex CLI and account. Used history covers only confirmed resets in Sparebar on this Mac.

This is a preview release. Real redemption is left to the user's explicit action. Launch at login after a real login, physical sleep/wake, VoiceOver, and sustained battery use still need manual verification.
