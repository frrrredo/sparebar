Sparebar keeps itself fresh - one less thing to remember.

New versions download in the background and install when you quit. Sparebar never restarts on its own.

A small orange dot lets you know an update is available. Open Sparebar to see what's new and choose Update and Restart whenever it suits you.

Prefer to choose each download? Turn off Automatic updates in Settings. Sparebar will still check once a day and let you know when something new is ready.
