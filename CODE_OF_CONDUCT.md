# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information are not welcome.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant and constructive. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Follow [GitHub's community guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).

Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for private reports of harassment. Do not repeat private information in a public issue.
